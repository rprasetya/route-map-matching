Snap GPS traces to the roads they were driven on.

Quick start in JavaScript, TypeScript or PHP

Give it road lines and GPS points; get back the path on the road, point by point. The npm package runs from plain JavaScript with import or require, from a script tag with no build step, and ships its own TypeScript types.

How a trace becomes a route

The same four stages run in both languages, tuned on trips from a production fleet tracker.

  1. Candidates

    Up to six road positions near each fix, scored by distance, heading, road class, one-way rules and the side traffic keeps to.

  2. Transitions

    A bounded Dijkstra search measures the road distance between candidates. Routes about as long as the straight GPS hop score best.

  3. Viterbi

    The cheapest chain of candidates across the whole trip wins, so one bad fix cannot drag the route onto a parallel street.

  4. Clean-up

    Gaps are routed, detours straightened and false U-turns removed, which keeps signal loss and multipath from showing up as zigzags.

Keeps left where traffic keeps left

On a divided road the two carriageways sit metres apart, closer than GPS noise. In the shaded countries the matcher leans toward the left lane, everywhere else it stays neutral. The boundaries come from Natural Earth and hold up at the Hong Kong and Shenzhen, Macau and Zhuhai, and Singapore and Johor borders.
